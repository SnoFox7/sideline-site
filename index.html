Sideline: Soccer

Live minutes, stats, and seasons for youth and college soccer coaches. Offline on iPhone. iOS 17 or later.

Sideline keeps the roster, matches, and seasons on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync. You can send a team or a live match to another phone when you choose to.
