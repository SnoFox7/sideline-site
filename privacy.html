Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026. This policy is for the iPhone app Sideline: Soccer (bundle ID sno.Soccer-Sideline).

Who we are

Sideline is operated by David Sno. Contact: snofox7@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Sideline is an offline live-stat console. Team names, player names, jerseys, minutes, stats, scores, seasons, and kit colors stay in an on-device SwiftData store. Settings such as haptics and hidden stat rows use on-device UserDefaults.

There is no account, no sign-in, no iCloud, and no backend.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, sell, or transmit personal data off the device. The app does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporters that upload, or tracking SDKs. The privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.

If you use Share (Game Card images, CSV spreadsheet, or text), iOS share sheet destinations you pick (Mail, Files, Messages, and so on) are under those apps’ policies. Sideline does not upload the file for you.

Children

Sideline is built for coaches and parents, not as a social network for children. Youth player names you enter stay on your device. We do not operate a server that receives children’s data.

How to delete data

Delete the Sideline app from the iPhone. That removes the on-device store. There is no cloud copy to request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will update. The live copy is always this URL.
